# Safe to Spend user test: session guide

The plan is in [Stage 2 §17.2](stage-2-architecture.md). It checks whether people read the figure correctly. **The formula is out of scope:** if a task fails, the wording or layout changes, and the people retesting are new ones.

**Status:** not run. Needs 5 people, recruited by the user.

## Who

5 people aged 18 or older, with no finance or accounting background, covering someone salaried (P1), someone paying down debt (P2) and someone with irregular income (P4). Tested in English. Other languages repeat this before they switch on.

## Before each session (30 minutes, in person or by video)

- Open the prototype. Press **Start over**, and choose **18 March**.
- Use invented data only. Nobody enters or shows their own finances. Stop anyone who starts to.
- Read the consent aloud, and get a written yes:

> We're testing an app, not you. You'll use a practice version with made-up money and say out loud what you think. I'll take notes under a code, not your name. I won't record anything unless you agree. You can stop at any time without saying why. Is that all right?

Then: "Please say what you're thinking as you go. If you get stuck, tell me what you'd expect. I can't explain the screen during the tasks, because that's what we're testing."

## Tasks

Ask each one as written, and don't explain terms. Note the person's own words.

| # | Day | Ask | A pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 18 March | "How much can you spend today without problems?" | Says €1,085, or about €155 a day |
| T2 | 18 March | "Why is this different from what your bank shows?" | Says it isn't the bank balance, and names one reason: bills, card debt or goal money |
| T3 | 18 March | "Until when is this amount meant to last?" | Says 24 March, or until the salary |
| T4 | 18 March | "What does the yellow line about rent mean?" | Understands the salary covers rent, so it isn't taken off yet |
| — | 18 March | "Show me what makes up this number." Start a timer | Opens "How it's calculated" within 30 seconds |
| T5 | Press **25 March**, then **26 March** | "Your salary is late today. What happens to the number?" | Says the number went down or that bills now count, and that the salary isn't counted until it arrives |
| T6 | Press **18 March** | "You paid a €150 bill from your holiday money. Record it." | Enters €150, chooses Holiday, and saves, with no help |

What should happen on T6: the figure stays at €1,085 and Holiday falls to €50. Answering No drops it to €935. Note which one they chose and whether they noticed.

## Passing

At least 4 of 5 people must pass each of: T1 with T3, T2, T4, finding the breakdown in time, and T6. T5 has no threshold in the plan, but a misunderstanding there is worth a wording change.

If a criterion fails, change the wording or layout, and retest with 2 or 3 new people.
